AI Technology

Risk scoring is the engine, not a dashboard feature.

Coverio is built around a purpose-trained risk-scoring engine — not a chatbot wrapper glued onto an underwriting dashboard. Every submission runs through the same model, trained on real claims outcomes, so two reviewers never get two different answers.
