Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Coverio Labs Ltd ("Coverio", "we", "us") provides an AI-assisted platform that helps insurers, MGAs, and brokers score and price risk. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, work email, organization name, and role.
  • Submission data you connect — applications, claims history, and inspection reports.
  • Usage data — how you interact with the product, used to improve reliability and features.
  • Technical data — device, browser, and log information collected to keep the service secure.

How we use your information

  • To score submissions for risk and suggest pricing tiers.
  • To generate portfolio and loss-ratio reports from your data.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve the platform.
  • To communicate about your account, updates, and support.

Data protection & security

Your submission data is yours. Each organization's data is isolated in its own workspace, encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell your data, and we do not use one organization's data to train models shared with another.

Submission data may contain personal information about policyholders or applicants. You are responsible for having the appropriate basis to collect and process that data before connecting it to Coverio. We apply role-based access controls, audit logging, and least-privilege practices across our infrastructure.

AI processing

Risk scoring and pricing suggestions are performed by AI models running on our secured cloud infrastructure. Scores are decision-support, not a final determination — a licensed underwriter should always review flagged submissions before a policy is issued.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us operate the platform (such as cloud hosting and analytics), under contracts that require them to protect it, and where required by law.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@coverio.io or write to Coverio Labs Ltd, Data Protection Office.
