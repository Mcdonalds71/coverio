Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: August 30, 2026 · Effective Immediately

1. Scope & Commitment to Carrier Confidentiality

Coverio Technologies Inc. ("Coverio", "we", "us") provides AI-powered document extraction and underwriting risk scoring for commercial insurance carriers, Managing General Agents (MGAs), reinsurers, and brokers. We treat all customer-submitted data (including ACORD applications, loss runs, engineering surveys, and insured financial records) with the highest tier of confidentiality.

2. Zero Model Retraining Guarantee

We explicitly warrant that data uploaded to or processed through Coverio is never used to train, fine-tune, or improve public or foundation machine learning models. Your submissions, claims loss histories, and proprietary rating algorithms remain strictly segregated within your dedicated tenant partition.

3. Data Encryption & Storage Architecture

All data in transit is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Data at rest is encrypted in Amazon S3 and Amazon RDS using AES-256 GCM encryption via customer-managed AWS Key Management Service (AWS KMS) keys.

4. Regulatory & NAIC Compliance

Coverio maintains adherence to National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC) model governance guidelines, state insurance department confidentiality mandates, SOC2 Type II compliance standards, and applicable state data protection frameworks (including CCPA/CPRA).

5. Contact Our Privacy Office

For questions regarding data processing agreements (DPA) or security audits, contact privacy@coverio.ai.
