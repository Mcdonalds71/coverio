Enterprise Governance

Security, Trust & Data Architecture.

Commercial insurance submissions contain confidential policyholder financials, employee census schedules, and sensitive loss runs. Coverio is architected from day one with institutional security controls.

01

Zero Model Retraining Policy

Coverio never uses your insured submissions, loss history files, or underwriting guidelines to train public or foundation AI models. Inference runs in dedicated, tenant-isolated private runtime containers with ephemeral memory caching.

02

Cryptographic KMS Isolation

All ingested documents are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest in Amazon S3 using AES-256-GCM encryption with client-specific AWS Key Management Service (AWS KMS) customer-managed keys (CMK).

03

SOC2 Type II & NAIC Governance Compliance

Coverio undergoes annual third-party SOC2 Type II audits covering Security, Availability, and Confidentiality. Our architecture strictly adheres to insurance model governance and auditability requirements.

04

Immutable Actuarial Audit Logs

Every risk extraction token, loss ratio probability calculation, and underwriter referral trigger is cryptographically hashed and logged to immutable WORM (Write Once, Read Many) cloud audit archives.
