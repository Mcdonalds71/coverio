Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Coverio pricing and deployment.

How are submission pages counted?

A submission packet typically consists of ACORD applications (4–12 pages), 5-year loss run spreadsheets, and engineering survey reports (20–60 pages). Each inbound submission file processed end-to-end counts as 1 submission.

What happens if we exceed our monthly submission volume?

Your pipeline is never throttled. Overage volume is billed transparently at your plan's discounted per-submission rate ($0.85 on Starter, $0.65 on Growth) at month end.

Can we connect Coverio into our existing Policy Admin System?

Yes. Coverio provides REST APIs, webhooks, and pre-built connectors for standard insurance platforms including Guidewire, Duck Creek, and custom broker intake portals.
