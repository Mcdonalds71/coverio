Our Origin

Built by commercial actuaries and cloud ML architects.

Coverio was founded to solve a critical enterprise bottleneck: commercial insurance underwriters drowning in 80-page unstructured submission packets, forced to choose between quoting fast and pricing true catastrophe risk.

By combining layout-aware Azure AI Document Intelligence with GPU-accelerated Bayesian loss ratio simulations, Coverio empowers carriers and MGAs to turn inbound broker queues into instant, profitable bound policies.
